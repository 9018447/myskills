# Glossary — Separation Process Principles (Seader, Henley & Roper, 4th ed.)

**Absorption factor (𝒜)** — L/(K·V); ratio of liquid to vapor carrying capacity; >1 favors absorption (Ch 6)
**Activity coefficient (γ)** — departure of liquid-phase fugacity from ideal solution behavior; =1 ideal (Ch 2)
**Azeotrope** — composition where yᵢ = xᵢ (Kᵢ=1); a distillation barrier; minimum-boiling most common (Ch 2, 11)
**Bow-tie region** — feasible product-composition region in azeotropic distillation; bounded by curved distillation boundaries (Ch 11)
**Breakthrough** — moment solute first appears in fixed-bed effluent; design time before it (Ch 15)
**Constant molar overflow** — assumption that L and V are constant in each section (equal latent heats); basis of McCabe–Thiele (Ch 7)
**Cut / cut point** — fraction of feed collected as permeate (membranes) or product fraction (batch) (Ch 13, 14)
**Distribution coefficient (K_D)** — x⁽¹⁾/x⁽²⁾ solute split between liquid phases; governs extraction (Ch 2, 8)
**Degrees of freedom (N_D)** — N_V − N_E design variables specifiable; Kwauk element-by-element assembly (Ch 4, 5)
**ESA vs MSA** — energy-separating agent (heat/work creates a phase) vs mass-separating agent (added selective phase; 4 penalties) (Ch 1)
**Extract / raffinate** — solvent-rich / feed-solvent-rich liquid phases in extraction (Ch 8)
**Exergy (availability)** — b = h − T₀s; maximum recoverable shaft work; LW = T₀ΔS_irr destroys it (Ch 2)
**Extraction factor (ℰ)** — K′_D·S/F_A; analog of 𝒜 for extraction cascades (Ch 5, 8)
**Fugacity / fugacity coefficient (φ̄ᵢ)** — pseudo-pressure for chemical potential; equal across phases at equilibrium (Ch 2)
**Hatta number (N_Ha)** — reaction rate / diffusion rate in reactive absorption; >1 means reaction-enhanced uptake (Ch 6)
**HETP / HETP (height equivalent to a theoretical plate)** — packed height per theoretical stage (Ch 6, 7)
**HTU–NTU** — H_OG·N_OG column-height decomposition for packed columns (Ch 6)
**K-value (Kᵢ = yᵢ/xᵢ)** — VLE ratio; from EOS (φ-φ), γ-φ, Raoult, or Henry models (Ch 2)
**Key components (light/heavy)** — the two components defining a column split; LLK/HHK non-keys distribute (Ch 9)
**Kremser equations** — closed-form fractional absorption/stripping φ for dilute cascades (Ch 6)
**LUB (length of unused bed)** — fixed-bed design length beyond stoichiometric front = MTZ/2 + margin (Ch 15)
**Maxwell–Stefan equations** — multicomponent diffusion framework with binary-pair coupling; basis of rate-based models (Ch 3, 12)
**MESH equations** — Material, Equilibrium, Summation, Heat balances per stage; N(2C+3) equations (Ch 10)
**Minimum reflux / pinch point** — R at which operating line touches equilibrium curve → infinite stages (Ch 7, 9)
**MTZ (mass-transfer zone)** — active region of a fixed bed where uptake occurs (Ch 15)
**Murphree efficiency (E_MV)** — actual vapor composition change / equilibrium change per tray (Ch 6, 7, 12)
**Osmotic pressure (π)** — RTc_B-driven pressure opposing solvent flow through membranes (Ch 14)
**Permeance / permeability** — membrane flux per driving force / per thickness; Barrer units (Ch 14)
**Pinch point** — zero driving-force point between operating and equilibrium lines (Ch 7, 9)
**Plait point** — ternary composition where two liquid phases become identical (Ch 4, 8)
**q-line** — feed thermal condition line y = q/(q−1)·x − z_F/(q−1) on y–x diagram (Ch 7)
**Rachford–Rice equation** — single-root flash function f(Ψ)=0 for V/F (Ch 4)
**Rayleigh equation** — differential (batch) distillation integral relating W₀/W to composition (Ch 13)
**Relative volatility (α)** — Kᵢ/Kⱼ; separation impractical near 1.00; pressure-independent under Raoult (Ch 2)
**Residue curve / distillation curve** — composition path of a still pot / total-reflux column; defines distillation regions (Ch 11)
**Selectivity (β)** — ratio of distribution coefficients; the solvent-selection criterion over capacity (Ch 8)
**Separation factor** — product of split ratios defining achievable sharpness of any separator (Ch 1)
**Solution–diffusion model** — membrane transport P_M = solubility × diffusivity (Ch 14)
**Stable node / saddle / unstable node** — singular points of residue-curve maps; classify azeotrope behavior (Ch 11)
**Solutropy** — reversal of tie-line direction with concentration; check before Hunter–Nash (Ch 8)
**Stripping factor (𝒮)** — K·V/L; >1 favors stripping (Ch 6)
**Tie line** — equilibrium composition pair on a binodal curve (Ch 4, 8)
**Tridiagonal (Thomas) algorithm** — O(N) solution of M-equation matrix; core of BP/SR methods (Ch 10)
**Underwood equations** — minimum-reflux roots θ for multicomponent Class 1/2 splits (Ch 9)
**warp/warped time (ξ)** — transformed time in Doherty–Perkins residue-curve DAE (Ch 11)
